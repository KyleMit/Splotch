predicate, expected positive, intended rejecting control and claim limits. Check real boundaries
before expanding fixtures. Ask Claude for plausible false passes and false rejections. Unexpected
setup failures are preserved separately and never count as rejecting controls.

actual conversation identity and review provenance before cleanup. Reconcile moving main at

acceptance. A retained/hybrid result remains incomplete until its applicable final gates pass. A
