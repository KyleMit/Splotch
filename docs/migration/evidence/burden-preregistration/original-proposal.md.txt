# Common-horizon burden preregistration proposal

This is a decision-policy plan for Claude challenge before documentation implementation or any remedy/comparative-result execution. Parent owns the final registration. No destination, recurring saving or performance benefit is selected. It will be appended to the one live CAMPAIGN register after review; original ranges and subsequent reviewed amendments remain visible.

The common horizon is 24 months beginning at the reviewed foundation decision. One effort-week means one person's concentrated engineering effort including implementation, validation and review, independent of elapsed waiting or agent concurrency. These are coarse estimates, not measured hours. Calendar delay consumes horizon; a retired owner's credit begins only at actual retirement. Compare all arms using the same units and category definitions. Do not annualize source-change counts into future hours or savings.

## Deployment and materiality policy

The deployment-delay planning range is 0–6 months for retention, 3–12 for native-paper hybrid, and 6–18 for each whole native/UI replacement. These are estimates anchored to unfulfilled obligations, not delivery promises. Calendar waiting is not engineering effort. Credit reduced obligation only for the actual remaining horizon after the old owner is retired. During transition, register both owners and actual synchronization/release duties. No assumed simultaneous cutover or zero overlap.

The contract's burden-only predicate governs: remove at least one demonstrated recurring obligation, with no uncompensated regression in any mandatory category, and require lower-bound recurring saving to exceed upper-bound remaining initial migration/validation plus risk reserve. With these initial overlapping estimate-only ranges and zero retired owners, no burden-only migration qualifies. This is not an architecture verdict; ordinary matched performance eligibility could still justify a candidate under the contract, with added burden explicitly accounted for.

Reconsider only uncertainty that could change the decision. Examples: real neutral/shared web adapter proof determines whether UI synchronization is one vocabulary or two; actual production-hybrid collector/backend/host contracts determine bridge maintenance; effective released tool/lock/floor execution determines native upkeep; retirement proof identifies which operational owner truly disappears. Revise a range only for reviewed new scope or feasibility evidence, preserving the prior range, reason and source. Do not shrink reserve or reset the horizon to make a preferred arm qualify.

Before timed candidate comparison, register separately the within-OS common visible/readiness observable, complete matched screen/prior journey, observer coverage and material performance threshold from fresh physical variability and owning action budgets. These estimates cannot substitute for those gates. Remedies and comparison results remain ineligible until this burden policy is reviewed and committed. Structural compilation and independent host/source tests remain eligible.
