# DS-2 slice: iOS simulator drawing-screen interaction and motion, 2026-10-19

Refs #2978. This is a diagnostic slice of DS-2 (`../execution-plan.md`), not a DS-2 run. It verifies
no requirement row and changes no status in `requirements.csv` or `protocol-mapping.csv`. Its output
is the defects it found, filed as issues, and a list of motion differences that still need a triage
against current shipping.

## What ran

* **`rn`:** the RN Release simulator build of 00fcc5c78d1031873db1a214301c008daa4bd080, bundle ID
  `art.splotch.migration.probe`. Binary SHA-256 `a9abc80fd9fc59e5…`.
* **`ref160`:** the reconstructed v1.6.0 reference; store binary source unknown. Source
  6572ed535f243d06e3f7bcaa5986218cac006d86, bundle ID `art.splotch.app.baseline.release`. Binary
  SHA-256 `cfbf1deb3b51bb1c…`, the same artifact as the DS-1 slice.
* **Target:** `iphone-notch` (iPhone 16e), a fresh iOS 26.5 (23F77) simulator with the status bar
  frozen at 9:41.
* **Axes:** Raised, light, portrait, in-app motion on. Each arm was installed fresh and launched
  once; the steps then ran in order on that launch.
* **Driver:** XCUITest W3C actions through Appium, the DS-1 slice's primitives. Controls were found
  by their shipping accessible names; both arms expose the same names for every control used, so no
  alias was needed.
* **Capture:** a `simctl` screen recording per step, from before its first input until the settle
  rule held, then the settled still and the accessibility tree. Every step on both arms settled.
  Motion was measured from the recordings resampled to 60 fps: a burst is a run of frames that
  change more than 0.08% of the scaled screen, closed by a 120 ms gap.

## Defects

* **#3012: Undo at end of history plays no cue.** `rn` makes the tile a disabled button once the
  history is empty, so the tap never reaches it. Shipping keeps it `aria-disabled` and replays
  `replayActionUnavailableFeedback` (`web/src/lib/components/UndoButton.svelte`). Its recording on
  `rn` holds one frame; `ref160` moves for 333 ms.
* **#3013: a triple tap on Clear shows no coachmark.** The same three 40 ms taps, 120 ms apart, show
  the coachmark on `ref160` and nothing on `rn`. Taps 200 ms apart also show nothing on `rn`. A
  single 700 ms hold does show it on `rn`, and `rn`'s tap-run rule matches shipping's, so the taps
  probably never reach the gesture's `down`.

## Motion durations: not judged

DS-2 passes timing within two recorded frames of the reference. Most cues ran longer on `rn` than on
`ref160`. The menus, the picker and the undo cues took 100 to 270 ms longer, and the drawer open and
the Purple pick about the same. Current shipping retuned its motion after v1.6.0, and `rn` ports
current shipping, so these differences cannot be judged against `ref160` alone. They need the DS-1
slice's triage arm, `web-prod` at the same viewport, before any is called a defect.
