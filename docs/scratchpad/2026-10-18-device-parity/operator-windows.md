# Bounded operator windows

Refs #2978 and #2690. This checklist prepares later physical work; it is not a grant record, a Ready
signal or evidence that a physical property passed. Detailed target identities, artifacts,
recordings and action records stay in byte-verified private local recovery. The protected operator
TSV is never staged, reverted or published.

## Admission before requesting Ready

* Freeze the reviewed candidate and qualify the exact baseline and candidate package IDs, source
  bindings, artifact bytes and signatures. Obtain any additional physical baseline authorization
  against a concrete prepared artifact before installation.
* Finish harmless public navigation, fixture and capture dry runs. Prove the driver's stroke
  cardinality with a meaningful negative control before trusting drawing, history or performance.
* Rediscover reservations, process ownership, ports and forwards using the capture-session method.
  Borrowed historical PIDs give no authority. Preserve the Galaxy stay-awake value of 15 and bind
  its observations to Display0.
* Name the mutable device and app in the availability request. Retained apps and their simulator
  remain read-only. Confirm no credential-entry capture is running.
* Divide work into batches of at most 600 seconds. Every cell has a setup, observable assertion,
  actual foreground check before input, checkpoint and restoration step. Preserve failed takes;
  retry only after diagnosis or corrected setup. Recheck build/source dependencies after a merge.

## Ready and automation grant

When the operator reports Ready, launch the prepared WDA within 30 seconds. Record the actual launch
and grant times privately. The operator unlocks the named mutable device and grants the requested
existing automation access. Reprove readiness and foreground identity; a connected cable or a
successful simulator session is not a physical grant. Do not ask the operator to confirm a screen
from a screenshot.

## Existing credentials and live AI

If a live flow requires an existing key or access code, the operator enters it directly on the named
mutable device. Suspend screenshots, video, accessibility-tree collection and input tracing during
entry. Never read, print, copy, provision or include the credential in a recovery package. Resume
only after the entry UI is dismissed and check the public masked saved-key state without exposing
the value.

The live picture-report test **writes to the real moderation queue**. Use an appropriate harmless
synthetic test picture and the real picture-report path; a problem report is not a substitute.
Record server acceptance separately from the confirmation layout. Refusal, timeout, retries,
retention and attempt 33 are first exercised with explicitly labeled fixtures through production
components and native adapters. Do not provoke unsafe live output.

Before every possible paid dispatch, require the zero-cost hosted capability/CORS/CSRF gate,
qualified public entry and harmless payload admission. Charge atomically in the persistent campaign
ledger before dispatch, including an ambiguous dispatch. The cumulative allowance starts at one
possible generation charged, zero returned pictures and zero accepted reports; it never resets by
session or device. Allocate the remaining nine possibilities across the two native success/report
flows and required RN-web coverage, preserving a diagnosed-retry reserve. No generation is spent on
attempt 33 or duplicated visual axes. A missing capability or exhausted allowance remains blocked.

## Checkpoint and restoration

At each cell, record the actual outcome, source/build/OS, state axes, assertion, inspector and any
failure. Save original media and traces privately and verify recovery bytes. Restore the mutable
font, motion, sound, permission and network settings changed in that batch. Release only owned
sessions, processes, forwards and reservations, preserving foreign services, retained apps and the
Galaxy stay-awake value. Report the resulting rig state and any unresolved observation. Epic #2690
stays open for the cutover decision.
