# DS-1 slice: iOS simulator drawing-screen chrome, 2026-10-19

Refs #2978. This is a diagnostic slice of DS-1 (`execution-plan.md`), not a DS-1 run. It verifies no
requirement row and changes no status in `requirements.csv` or `protocol-mapping.csv`. Its output is
a defect list ranked by user impact, for fix units to take.

## What ran

* **`rn`:** the RN Release simulator build, captured at db4ef5393d2424bbc22668c339353a3ab309aacf.
  The base has since moved to 5aae305c99ed7db8472a5079b6595781a2e80465, which changes only
  `docs/migration/CONTRACT.md`.
* **`ref160`:** the reconstructed v1.6.0 reference; store binary source unknown. Source
  6572ed535f243d06e3f7bcaa5986218cac006d86, bundle ID `art.splotch.app.baseline.release`.
* **Triage arm:** `web-prod`, shipping source dd6b86a2343d400bb654bc664020c09768e6c7f5, as a static
  export in Playwright WebKit. It runs at each simulator's viewport and DPR, with that simulator's
  safe-area insets injected through the shipping `--safe-area-inset-*` seam: 32/0/20/0 pt (top,
  right, bottom, left) on the iPad, and on the iPhone 47/0/34/0 pt in portrait and 0/47/21/47 pt in
  landscape.
* **Targets:** `iphone-notch` (iPhone 16e) and `ipad-13` (13-inch iPad Pro), fresh iOS 26.5 (23F77)
  simulators with the status bar frozen at 9:41.
* **Axes:** Raised/Flat × light/dark × portrait/landscape on `rn` and `web-prod`. On `ref160` the
  axes are Raised only, because v1.6.0 predates the Button style setting.
* **States:** S1, S2 and S3; the open "Stroke width" menu for Pen and for Eraser; S6 in phone
  landscape; the S7 picker; and S12.

Every still met the plan's settle rule: three byte-identical frames, all at least 1,000 ms after the
last input. The S12 stroke driver passed the cardinality check: three strokes gave three Undos, and
the joined negative control gave one.

### Departures from DS-1

* The plan pairs `web-prod` with `rn-web`. Pairing it with native `rn` is a triage aid only, used to
  tell post-v1.6.0 shipping changes apart from RN deviations.
* Not captured: S0 recordings, the per-size stills S4.1 to S5.5, S8 to S11, S13, and the V1 to V5
  variants. S12 used the "Cat coloring page" rather than the first Farm page.
* `ipad-mini` was not run.
* `android-api36` was skipped. The host had 7.1 GB free, below the campaign's 10 GB floor for
  emulators. The emulator's first boot failed with no space for its userdata partition. Both APKs
  were built, but no Android cell exists.

## Defects

Ranked by user impact. Each defect reproduced in every listed cell and is absent from both
references wherever that reference has the cell.

### D1: the Stroke width glyph vanishes while the Color Menu is open

In phone landscape, opening the Color Menu removes the "Stroke width" tile's three-wave glyph. Only
a fragment of the bottom wave, about 6 pt long, remains. Shipping keeps the glyph and dims it. The
same tile renders correctly with the brush or width menu open, and in S2.

* **Cells:** `iphone-notch`, S6, in all four style × theme cells.
* **Pair:** `iphone-notch`, `raised-light-landscape`, `s06-color-menu`, `rn` × `web-prod`.
* **Repro:** fresh install, landscape, Pen. Tap "Expand controls", then tap "Colors". The capture
  ran the brush and width menus first, as the state list orders them. The collapsed drawer is
  untested.

### D2: the selected swatch ring is drawn with a halo

Both references draw the selected swatch as a flat gap of bar surface and a crisp ring. RN shades
the band between the ring and the paint. The worst channel delta inside the ring is 104 to 124/255
in dark and 67 to 93/255 in light.

* **Cells:** both targets, Raised and Flat, both themes, in every state with a ringed swatch.
* **Pair:** `ipad-13`, `raised-dark-portrait`, `s01-blank`, `rn` × `web-prod`. The same ring on
  `ref160` (`iphone-notch`, `raised-dark-portrait`, `s01-blank`) is crisp too.
* **Repro:** fresh install, any orientation with the palette bar visible; look at the selected
  Purple swatch.
* **Unconfirmed lead:** `experiments/native-architecture/src/rsdDrawing/RsdSwatch.tsx` keeps the
  swatch's `colors.floatShadow` while the selected disc takes the surface colour.

### D3: the Clear button is clipped at the paper edge in phone landscape

RN clips the 70 pt "Clear drawing" button at the paper's right edge, so only 60 pt shows. Shipping
sets `right: calc(-10px + var(--safe-area-right))`, so the button extends 10 pt into the coloured
inset band. The accessibility frames are identical; only the visible extent differs.

* **Cells:** `iphone-notch`, every landscape state, in all four style × theme cells.
* **Pair:** `iphone-notch`, `raised-light-landscape`, `s01-blank`, `rn` × `web-prod`.
* **Repro:** fresh install, landscape; look at the Clear button in the top-right corner.

### D4: the phone-landscape action row is off shipping's spacing

With the drawer expanded in phone landscape, "Save screenshot" starts at 113 pt and "Undo" at 171
pt, against shipping's 115 and 175 pt. "Collapse controls" starts at 229 pt against 227 pt. RN
spaces the row with even 10 pt gaps. Shipping uses 12 pt between the tiles and 4 pt before the
chevron. Only Undo is more than 2 pt off.

* **Cells:** `iphone-notch`, landscape, from S2 on, in all four style × theme cells.
* **Pair:** `iphone-notch`, `raised-light-landscape`, `s02-drawer-open`, `rn` × `web-prod`.
* **Repro:** fresh install, landscape; tap "Expand controls".

## Accepted differences and noise

* **Settled:** the X44 bottom-inset trim, where the iPad in landscape shows 13 swatches and shipping
  web shows 14 running into the bottom inset. Also the iOS blur behind the picker, and the iOS top
  band coloured from launch.
* **Reference drift, where `rn` matches shipping main:** the phone-landscape Color Button flyout;
  the bottom offsets of the toolbar, drawer and Settings, 34 pt on the iPhone and 20 pt on the iPad;
  the v1.6.0 palette spacing; and the S12 page placement. Shipping changed its iOS WebView inset
  handling and its phone-landscape colour access after v1.6.0.
* **Comparator noise:** simulator status-bar content, and the iPadOS window-resize grip that both
  native arms show. Web's accessibility tree also exposes the toolbar behind the open picker. The
  Flat palette's "surface" sample, 3 to 5/255 off, lands on paper grain, which the plan judges from
  the originals. Also crayon grain, and sub-point rasterization fringes on glyph strokes and flyout
  keylines.
* **Within tolerance:** the portrait "Stroke width" middle wave is about 1 pt thinner.

Everywhere else in the slice, `rn` matched `web-prod` within 2 pt and 2/255. That includes every
control frame in the phone and iPad portrait cells, and every S7 hexagon frame. The S12 page
placement matched, and its ink matched on inspection of the originals.
