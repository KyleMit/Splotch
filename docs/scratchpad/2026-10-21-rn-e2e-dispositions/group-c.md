# RN E2E dispositions, group C (`install-banner` to `web-back`)

Issue #3156, part of epic #3148. ADR-0182 gives the rule: port a SvelteKit E2E spec when it tests
behaviour the React Native app has and nothing else covers it; otherwise delete it with a recorded
reason, either "covered by X" or "Svelte-only: why".

Each of the 40 specs was decided one at a time, against the running RN web export. A port was run
before it was kept: its first run showed what the export does, and an assertion the export cannot
meet was never written down as a pass. Where the export lacks something the SvelteKit app had, the
row says "RN gap", the spec does not assert it, and the gap is parked for the owner.

| Outcome              | Specs |
| -------------------- | ----- |
| Ported               | 27    |
| Deleted, covered     | 5     |
| Deleted, Svelte-only | 8     |

The ports live in `e2e/specs/<name>.spec.ts`, share `e2e/support/group-c.ts`, and run in Chromium
unless the row says they also run in Firefox and WebKit. No spec in this group is in the SvelteKit
suite any more. The deletions left some `web/tests` helper exports with no importer (knip's
`lint:dead` is a CI gate): `enterFullscreen`, `exitFullscreen`, `resizeInFullscreen`,
`IPHONE_CHROME_UA`, `alphaAt` and `seedInstallStage` are deleted, and `SETTINGS_FILL_FRAME_BUDGET`,
`settleSettingsPane` and `BANNER_MOUNT_TIMEOUT_MS` lost their `export` because a surviving helper
still uses them.

## RN gaps and observations for the owner

Each row is something the SvelteKit app did that the RN export does not, or a flaw found while
porting. None is asserted by a spec.

1. **Open tool menus ignore a mouse.** On the web export a mouse press or stroke outside an open
   Brushes, Stroke width or Colors menu leaves it open; touch closes it. The close is
   `useFlyoutSlot`'s `capturePress` in `RsdActionsPanel.tsx`. Why a mouse press never reaches it was
   not isolated; the paper's `pointerdown` handler in `PaperInputView.web.tsx` calls
   `preventDefault()`, which is the first place to look.
2. **Scroll fades.** The standalone pages (`/feedback`, `/changelog`, `/beta`) draw no fade where
   content runs past the fold; the Settings fade covers the 15px classic scrollbar; and the Settings
   fade fades in after open instead of being there at the first frame.
3. **Outbound policy links.** On `/privacy` the links that leave the app have no external mark and
   no "(opens outside Splotch)" accessible name, and "Send us a note" is a 20px text link.
4. **`theme-color`.** After an in-app visit to a page the tag keeps the drawing colour, and
   standalone pages keep `#ffffff` under a dark OS.
5. **Introduction before scripts.** On a short window the About this page introduction is hidden,
   with no toggle, until scripts have run.
6. **Back after a reload.** Drawing, Settings, About, Privacy Policy, reload, Back: the URL becomes
   `/` and the privacy page stays.
