# RN web E2E dispositions, group B (issue 3155)

The 41 SvelteKit specs from `engine-resize.spec.ts` through `install-banner-layout.spec.ts` in
`web/tests/`, each decided on its own under ADR-0182's rule: **port** a behaviour the RN app has and
nothing else covers; otherwise **delete** with a recorded reason, either "covered by <path>" or
"Svelte-only: <why the RN app has no such behaviour>".

Every ported spec lives in `e2e/specs/` and drives the exported app as a person does: no `/dev/*`
routes and no `window.__splotch*` hooks. Where a spec needs a deterministic AI answer it stubs
`/api/generate-image` or `/api/report-image` with `page.route`; no spec reaches a model provider.
The helpers the ports share are in `e2e/support/group-b.ts`.

## RN gaps and parked questions

None of these has a ported spec: a spec that fails against the app is not committed.

1. **A keyboard pick leaves focus on `body`** (`flows-actions-panel.spec.ts`). Choosing Crayon or a
   stroke size with Enter closes the flyout but focus lands on `body`, where shipping returns it to
   the button that opened the flyout. `native-flyout-focus.test.mjs` asserts the return and passes
   in happy-dom; the real browser disagrees. Escape does return focus (ported).
2. **Picking a colour leaves the Brush menu open** (`flows-palette-brush.spec.ts`). Shipping closes
   an open Brush menu when a swatch is picked. RN keeps it open until the next tap elsewhere.
3. **A Brush menu left open under a keyboard-opened Settings comes back**
   (`flows-optional-brushes.spec.ts`). With the menu open, opening Settings from the keyboard,
   changing the brush switches and closing it, shipping leaves the menu shut; RN shows the old menu
   again. The parity ledger recorded this as low severity.

Not recorded as gaps: the 404 page's missing `noindex` meta (the status already keeps it out of an
index), and the tap about 50 ms after the picker opens (see `flows-coloring-book-picker.spec.ts`).
