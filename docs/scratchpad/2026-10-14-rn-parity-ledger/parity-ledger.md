## Summary

### How confident can we be?

| Dimension                               | Confidence           | One-line basis                                                                                                                                           |
| --------------------------------------- | -------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Feature                                 | **Moderate**         | Every shipping surface has an RN counterpart, but about a dozen user-facing capabilities are missing or narrower, and RN adds several shipping never had |
| Visual (still frames)                   | **Moderate to good** | The best-guarded dimension: tokens, icons, geometry ladders, copy, paper grain and polaroid keyframes are read from shipping by drift tests              |
| Visual (motion and transitional states) | **Low**              | Most chrome motion is absent; the toolbar dims during routine work; the paper lifts on layout changes                                                    |
| Logic                                   | **Low**              | Most logic is reimplemented with no behavioural guard, and the code read found about 100 concrete behaviour differences                                  |
| Persistence within RN                   | **Good**             | RN remembers the same choices across relaunches, with four small gaps                                                                                    |
| Persistence across an in-place upgrade  | **None**             | RN reads no shipping store and no import path exists                                                                                                     |

**Feature.** Every shipping surface has an RN counterpart:

* all 12 Settings sections, about 50 rows, and the same defaults;
* the full drawer, palette, picker, Clear and Save Picture set;
* coloring books;
* AI Art end to end;
* the Grown-Ups Only check and Parent Center.

The gaps are real but bounded:

* saving on Android 7–10;
* coloring downloads that survive backgrounding;
* pinch-to-enlarge text in Settings, and pinch-zoom on the AI result;
* hiding the AI button offline;
* the clear-drag sounds;
* hardware-keyboard undo and Escape on native;
* wake lock;
* the PWA update flow and folder save (RN web only);
* a root error screen.

RN also adds behaviour shipping does not have. Each is a product decision rather than parity, and
none has been signed off:

* the last picture comes back after a relaunch;
* AI runs recover after an app kill;
* six custom colours are remembered on the bar;
* a picture-size cap ("This picture is full").

**Visual.** Still frames should look close: tokens, icons, geometry ladders, copy, paper grain and
polaroid keyframes are read from shipping by drift tests. These are not guarded:

* **Motion**, mostly absent: drawer cascade, menu entrance and exit, swatch bloom, Undo ghost and
  spin, camera squash, page-art land and lift, Settings section reveal.
* **Chrome dimming:** the chrome greys out during saves, clears, settings writes and page loads, and
  behind every dialog.
* **Native Flat** has no frosted glass.
* **The fit-only paper** shrinks and lifts the picture on layout changes.
* **Palette contents** change once custom colours are picked, and iPad landscape shows one colour
  fewer.
* **A theme flash** appears on native cold start.
* **OS font scaling** may enlarge RN text (unverified).

Expect a screenshot sweep to look close and a screen recording not to.

**Logic.** RN imports 41 distinct shipping modules (27 ledger rows after grouping); everything else
is reimplemented.

* **Weak guards.** Of the 118 module rows classed "ported with drift guard", about 40 pin only
  constants, copy or geometry, not behaviour. The 1,526-line shipping engine has no behavioural
  guard.
* **RN tests pin the divergence.** At least 18 RN tests assert the *opposite* of shipping's
  behaviour, pinning RN's divergence as intended (section 1.2).
* **Tests on unreachable code.** 27 RN test files mount the unreachable legacy `DrawingScreen.tsx`
  or dead AI views, so a green RN suite proves less about the live screen than it appears to.
* **Unguarded differences.** The slices found about 100 concrete behaviour differences, about 30 of
  them high, nearly all in code no test compares.

The device passes kept finding behaviour differences incidentally. The modules in section 1.3
explain why, and that pattern will continue until those modules get two-sided tests or are imported
rather than ported.

**Persistence.** Within RN, a relaunch keeps the same choices shipping keeps, and colour stays
session-only as in shipping. The four gaps:

* per-section "seen" stamps;
* last network state;
* the free-generation badge hint;
* pictures waiting for a save retry.

Across an in-place upgrade nothing survives (section 2.4). RN also persists data shipping never
kept, which raises storage-growth and privacy-review questions:

* the current picture;
* an AI attempt journal capped at 32 attempts;
* a JSON copy of every AI original, in iCloud-backed `Documents` on iOS and up to 8 MB per
  `localStorage` entry on web;
* the dismissed AI result.

* **Sound:** the Clear drag is silent (no water-drop ladder or cancel unwind), and the iOS silent

### 1.2 How much the guards and RN tests prove

* **Imports are few.** RN imports 41 distinct shipping modules (27 ledger rows after grouping). The
  imports are mostly constants and pure geometry: `breakpoints`, `design/tokens`, `hexPickerLayout`,
  `glassPaneGeometry`, `trimGeometry`, `platform/notchBand`, `platform/reducedMotion`,
  `ai/generationAttempt`, `storageKeys`, `idbDatabase`. No stateful store, the engine, storage or
  the gate is imported.
* **About 40 of 118 guarded rows are partial.** The guard pins constants, copy, labels or geometry
  while the behaviour around them is unguarded. Examples: `state/colors` (ink constants only),
  `ScreenshotButton` (aria-label only), `saveOnDelete` (copy only), `drawing/imageSave` (album name
  only), `ErrorScreen` (copy, not triggers).
* **At least 18 RN tests assert the opposite of shipping's behaviour:**
  * `native-appearance-paint`: ink re-themes;
  * `native-custom-colors` and `rsd-native-toolbar`: six remembered paints on the bar;
  * `rsd-native-stroke-chrome`: chrome refuses mid-stroke;
  * `rsd-native-drawer-style-switch`: chrome fades behind dialogs;
  * `native-control-preferences`: whole-record rejection;
  * `native-save-to-photos-mounted`: a failed Auto-Save keeps the drawing;
  * `native-photo-library`: Android below 11 is "unavailable";
  * `rsd-native-settings-info`: Privacy and changelog gated and external;
  * `native-feedback-transport`: server error text replaced;
  * `native-offline-library`: downloads pause in the background;
  * `rsd-native-coloring-picker:352`: Back appears mid-open;
  * `native-web-service-worker`: registration at window load;
  * `rsd-native-notch-band`: icon style follows the app theme;
  * `aiStorage.web.test.mjs`: the 33rd AI attempt is rejected;
  * `rsd-native-drawing-frame`: iPad landscape trim excludes the bottom inset.

  Each is a deliberate-looking choice no one compared against shipping. Each should be reopened or
  recorded as an accepted divergence.
* **27 RN test files mount the unreachable legacy `DrawingScreen.tsx`** (through
  `tools/tests/native-drawing-screen-harness.mjs`) **or dead AI views** (`NativeAiPictureView`,
  `AiPictureViewport`, `useAiPictureShare`). They still prove shared hooks, models and product

### 2.4 If RN replaced shipping in place under `art.splotch.app`, what would an existing install lose?

**Everything the app keeps, except saved pictures and the free-generation allowance:**

* **Settings:** theme, sound and volume, Button Size, toolbar style, every Tool Drawer and Saving
  switch, orientation lock, Apple Pencil choice, Reduce Motion. They reset to defaults.
* **Parent Center:** every policy resets. On native that means Every time, which is the conservative
  direction, but a parent who chose Per session or Never will be asked again. Section "seen" state
  and the session count reset too.
* **AI:**
  * AI Art turns off;
  * the parent's API key and managed access code are not found, because the Keychain and Keystore
    names differ;
  * the customization and auto-save choices reset;
  * the badge hint is lost.
* **Coloring:** downloaded books are not found and must be downloaded again; the mobile-data choice
  resets.
* **Pictures waiting for a save retry** are lost. They are in WebView IndexedDB and RN keeps such
  pictures only in memory.
* **The current drawing:** nothing to lose, because shipping never kept it.
* **Saved pictures** survive: they are in the OS photo library.
* **Free-generation allowance:** survives on native if the same Apple Team ID and Android signing
  key are used, because the identity derivation is identical.

**Is there an import or migration path today? No.**

* No RN code references `CapacitorStorage`, `WSSecureStorageSharedPreferences`,
  `capacitor-storage_`, `splotch-secure`, `splotch-unsaved-pictures`, `noBackupFilesDir/coloring` or
  `Application Support/coloring`.
* RN's own "legacy" and "migrate" code (`soundSettingsCodec.ts`, `model.ts`) upgrades RN's earlier
  record versions only.
* `experiments/native-architecture/OFFLINE-LIBRARY.md` states that no legacy data or jobs are
  imported.
* The `claude/native-beta-import` branch is already merged into this base and is unrelated.
* The RN projects still use `art.splotch.migration.probe` for the bundle id, the Android
  `applicationId` and namespace, and the Kotlin package path.

A cutover therefore needs an importer that runs once on first launch. It would read:

1. iOS `UserDefaults` `CapacitorStorage.*`, or Android `CapacitorStorage.xml`, for settings and gate
   policies;
2. the KeychainSwift items with account `capacitor-storage_<name>` (decoding the JSON-quoted value),
   or Android `WSSecureStorageSharedPreferences` plus the per-secret Keystore keys;
3. the coloring pack directories (adopt verified books or delete them);
4. `splotch-unsaved-pictures`, only if WebView IndexedDB can be reached from native code (on iOS it
   lives in WKWebsiteDataStore, which is impractical), so in practice those pictures are lost.

## Appendix: method and limits

* **Slices:** five read-only analysts each covered one slice of `web/src/lib/` against the RN tree
  at the pinned commit:
  * engine;
  * chrome (palette, drawer, Clear, Save Picture);
  * Settings, Grown-Ups Only and feedback;
  * AI Art;
  * coloring, saving, install, errors and platform.

  Each produced a module ledger, a behaviour inventory and concrete differences with file:line
  evidence (`slices/`).
* **Merging and re-checks:** the orchestrator merged the tables into the CSVs and covered the
  modules outside the slices: storage plumbing, web-only pages, admin, styleguide, beta, server and
  icons. It also re-read both sides for every claim marked "Verified".
* **Guard detection:** started from every string literal in an RN test that resolves to a shipping
  `web/src` file. Each slice then confirmed the test actually compares both sides. `code-map-rules`,
  `cut-release` and `gen-release-notes` were discarded as false positives.
* **Limits:**
  * Nothing was run. Device-only effects are marked: iOS silent switch, palm behaviour, font
    scaling, dark cover filters on iOS, the CSRF 403 against production.
  * Line numbers are at 64a9ca53a, and some RN branches in flight
    (`claude/native-chrome-behind-dialogs`, `claude/native-flat-panes`,
    `claude/native-flat-adopted-sheet`) are not included.
  * `docs/migration/` was deliberately not read.
