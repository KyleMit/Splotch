## 3. Concrete differences

**D1 Undo depth (H).** Shipping keeps 20 steps (`web/src/lib/drawing/undoHistory.ts:6`
MAX_UNDO_DEPTH; patch budget `tiledHistoryLimits.ts:7-8`). RN keeps up to 50
(`drawing/model.ts:57`), trimmed by a 200k-point budget summed over full-drawing snapshots
(`model.ts:58,96-105`). Each undo entry holds every prior stroke, so a picture past ~10k points gets
fewer than 20 steps, and one near the 100k cap gets 1-2. No test pins either value.

**D2 Picture caps and error banners (H).** RN refuses a contact or sample past 1000 strokes or 100k
points with "This picture is full. Save it, then start a new picture." (`model.ts:36-38,114-124`;
`contactCohort.ts:47-49,67`). A single stroke past 100k points gets "This stroke is too long…"
(`model.ts:196-197`). Malformed touch history gives "That touch was interrupted. Lift and try
again." (`contactResponder.ts:43`). Every `report()` lands in the visible "Something went wrong"
failure banner (`drawingMessages.ts:29-33` → `rsdDrawing/RsdDrawingStatus.tsx:74-81`). Shipping has
no cap and no input error UI. A busy toddler can reach 1000 taps and strokes, since eraser strokes
count too.

**D3 Page change is always undoable (H).** Shipping records a page change as an undo step only when
retained Magic ink exists (`web/src/lib/drawing/coloringAppearance.ts:15-35` →
`engine.ts:1176-1183`; `tiledMagicRecode.ts:85-95` returns false otherwise). With pen ink, or on a
blank page, Undo after picking a page removes the last stroke and keeps the page. RN commits every
page change to history (`useDrawingScreen.ts:234-246`, `model.ts:167-171`) and announces "…ready.
Undo brings your picture back.". So in RN, Undo lights up after a page pick on a blank page and
walks back through page swaps.

**D4 Second finger and chrome during a stroke (M-H, verify on device).** In shipping, any palette or
swatch press with a second finger ends every live stroke and picks the colour
(`components/ColorPalette.svelte:47-63,87`, `ColorSwatch.svelte:52-55`, `releaseAllPointers` at
`engine.ts:1092`). Drag-to-clear does the same (`actions/dragToClear.ts:189`). Undo works mid-stroke
without the ghost (`engine.ts:1139-1160`; engine-undo "undo preserves a stroke that is still in
progress").

RN native chrome is RN `Pressable` (`rsdDrawing/RsdPressable.tsx`), and the paper's PanResponder
refuses termination (`drawing/DrawingSurface.tsx:225-236`; the comment at
`useDrawingScreen.ts:85-90` says "The paper's responder keeps every touch until the lift"). So a
second-finger press on any control is rejected while a finger, or a resting palm, is on the paper.
On RN-web the press lands, but undo, clear, save, page picker and settings are refused while
`drawing` (`useDrawingScreen.ts:94-100,405-417`; `RsdEdgeControls.tsx:106-110`; flyouts
`RsdActionsPanel.tsx:68-70`), and a colour pick leaves the live stroke in the old colour. With no
palm rejection on either side, a resting palm in RN blocks the whole toolbar.

**D5 Paper presentation is fit-only (H).** Shipping picks between three presentations
(`web/src/lib/drawing/paperView.ts:89-114`):

* `adopt` when the canvas is empty, or on a large non-rotation resize.
* `fit` on rotation.
* `window` (identity crop) when the inked paper still covers a viewport that shrank by no more than
  chrome (≤96 px, `paperView.ts:44`).

RN always contain-fits an adopted paper and lifts it off a margin
(`drawing/paperGeometry.ts:76-108`). The paper slot shrinking for system bars, toolbar style (the
reported Flat→Raised lift), notch band, fullscreen exit or Split View therefore scales the picture
down. Shipping crops in place (engine-system-bars specs). Erasing to blank also leaves RN's paper
locked, while shipping re-adopts it (`engine.ts` setCanvasEmptyState).

**D6 Off-paper movement draws along the edge (M).** RN clamps every sample into the paper
(`drawing/paperSpace.ts:197` paperContactPoint; `model.ts:173-181`). A finger or Pencil that slides
past the paper edge and comes back paints a line hugging the border. Shipping keeps the captured
stroke going off-canvas and clips it, leaving a gap (engine-pointer-recovery:178, `gapAlpha 0`).

**D7 Input-hygiene guards absent (M).** Four shipping guards have no RN counterpart: the 100 ms
colour-change debounce for touch (`engine.ts:824-829`, `strokeMath.ts:143`), the edge-swipe guard
(`engine.ts:846-853`, `strokeMath.ts:65-125`), coalesced samples (`engine.ts:1016`), and
resumed-pointer restart (`strokeMath.ts:137-154`). `contactResponder.ts:246-308` admits every
contact. An OS-cancelled touch commits its partial ink (`DrawingSurface.tsx:158-159` →
`contactResponder.ts:452-461`), so a home-indicator swipe from the bottom can leave a stub stroke.

**D8 Geometry change ends the live stroke (M).** Any paper re-measure (onLayout, scroll on web)
calls `interrupt` → `resize()`, which commits the drafts and ignores every still-down contact
(`paperGeometry.ts:44-50`, `contactResponder.ts:476-483`, `DrawingSurface.tsx:114-116,200-212`).
Shipping keeps drawing through resize and rotation and undoes it as one unit (engine-resize:131;
settle in `engineListeners.ts:4`).

**D9 Input refused while things load or prepare (M).** RN disables the paper while any of these is
pending (`rsdDrawing/NativePaper.tsx:49-56`, `useDrawingScreen.ts:46,142`):

* draft recovery is loading;
* brush preferences or sound settings have not loaded;
* `preparing` is set.

`preparing` comes from SkiaInk `ready`, which needs the layout size and the decoded book fill
(`drawing/SkiaInk.tsx:248-266`), and from RasterInk checkpoint preparation on web
(`drawing/RasterInk.tsx:109-112,144-152`). The same flags dim the whole chrome
(`useDrawingScreen.ts:91-92`). Touches are dropped at launch, after every coloring-page pick (the
InkLayer remounts on its `pageId:theme` key, `DrawingSurface.tsx:161-163`), on a theme switch, and
on web around eraser/crayon selection. Shipping draws before hydration (`earlyBoot.ts:37`) and
recodes Magic ops recorded before the sheet arrives (`engine.ts` emptyScanAwaitsMagicReveal /
recodeMagicOpsToCurrentSheet).

**D10 Persisted current-picture draft (H, product decision).** RN writes the whole drawing (strokes,
page, Magic rainbow, adopted paper) after every change (`product/createDrawingDraft.ts:38-64`).
Native writes a verified file revision (`platform/drawingDraft.ts:48-83`); web uses IndexedDB with
cross-tab authority (`platform/drawingDraftOwner.web.ts`). At launch it restores that drawing with
an empty undo stack (`createDrawingDraft.ts:66-99`, `createHistory(drawing)`). User-visible
consequences:

* After an OS kill or relaunch, the child sees the old picture and coloring page rather than
  shipping's blank page.
* Undo is disabled even though ink is on the page; only Clear removes it.
* A picture adopted in the other orientation opens lifted and letterboxed.
* Drawing is refused until the restore finishes (D9).
* Failures show banners such as "Your current picture could not be recovered…" with "Retry draft
  recovery", and "…could not be saved. Keep this app open and retry.".
* A second browser tab can hit a draft conflict.
* Start over discards the draft (`product/DrawingSession.tsx:92-104`).
* Each stroke pays a full JSON encode, parse and write; large pictures risk jank.

**D11 Undo feedback missing (M).** Shipping's Undo is aria-disabled but still tappable: at end of
history it replays the unavailable cue, and every undo spins the icon
(`components/UndoButton.svelte:11-17,30,38`). The ghost drifts 28% toward the button
(`inkMotion.ts:15-18`, `engine.ts:1143-1150`). RN disables the button when history is empty and
undoes instantly with no ghost or spin (`rsdDrawing/RsdActionsPanel.tsx:255-266`).

**D12 Keyboard undo (M).** Shipping installs Ctrl/Cmd+Z on every build, Capacitor included
(`boot/undoShortcut.ts:17-31`, `routes/+page.svelte:178`). RN native is a no-op
(`platform/useUndoShortcut.ts:1`), so an iPad hardware keyboard cannot undo. RN-web adds blockers:
live stroke, busy/preparing, an open product modal, and empty history
(`product/useDrawingUndoShortcut.ts:8-15`, `RsdDrawingScreen.tsx:248-251`). native-undo-shortcut's
"keeps history unchanged while Drawing tools is open" exercises the OLD `DrawingScreen.tsx`, where
the menu takes an input lease.

**D13 Magic rainbow (M).** Shipping builds a random pool of 10 rainbows and picks one at random
(`magicSheetGradient.ts:12-46`, `magicBrush.ts:509`). RN uses a fixed formula
(`drawing/brushes.ts:15-41`). Index 0 comes first on every fresh drawing, then advances by 1 per
Clear (`model.ts:159-165`) and persists with the draft. The RN rainbows are narrower: always 7
stops, a 320° sweep, S84%, L60%.

**D14 Black ink re-themes (L-M).** Shipping resolves Black to white at draw time and stores the hex,
so drawn ink keeps its colour across a theme switch (`state/colors.svelte.ts:53-57`). RN stores
semantic `Black` and re-renders it per theme (`drawing/palette.ts:137-146`; the InkLayer remounts on
theme). Switching light→dark turns existing black strokes white, and a restored draft follows the
current theme.

**D15 Halos (L).** Shipping has one eraser bubble that also follows mouse or Pencil hover and shows
on pointerenter (`components/PointerHalos.svelte:41-58,230-236`). RN draws one bubble per accepted
contact and has no hover (`contactResponder.ts:99-116`).

**D16 Blank detection threshold (L).** Shipping counts alpha <4 on a 0.25× downscale as blank
(`emptyScan.ts:11,14`). RN applies alpha ≥4 at full resolution (`pngLimits.ts:1`,
`inkCoverage.ts:20-21`). Faint anti-aliased eraser fringes can keep RN "inked", which leaves the
screenshot camera enabled and records an undo step on Clear. No guard pins the scale.

**D17 Crayon pass details (L).** RN lacks the 64-move checkpoint (`crayonPassBoundaries.ts:22`). Its
anchor spacing is the 0.35-width direction step instead of 0.25 width, and it drops the
MIN_PROXIMITY_PX / MIN_EXCLUDE_ARC_DIR_STEPS floors (`crayonPassTracker.ts:26-42` vs
`drawing/crayon.ts:9-14,83`). Buildup can differ slightly on long or looping strokes. The only
"shipping crayon7" oracle is orphaned.

**D18 Page art motion (L-M).** Shipping's page lands with a fade and 1.025→1 scale, and lifts off
with a fade and 0.985 scale on removal (`components/DrawingCanvas.svelte:399-457`). RN's PageOutline
is static.

**D19 AI preview pinch-zoom missing (M, AI slice).** `actions/pinchZoom.svelte.ts` (1-4×, pan) has
no counterpart in `rsdDrawing/RsdAiResultStage.tsx`.

**D20 Upgrade migration (L-M).** Shipping persists brush and sizes under `splotch-brush-type`,
`splotch-stroke-width-size` and `splotch-eraser-width-size` (`storageKeys.ts:30-32`). RN never reads
them, so the brush resets to Pen and the sizes to medium on upgrade.

**D21 Clear is async and fallible (L-M).** RN captures and decodes the ink before clearing, and can
fail with "The picture changed before Clear finished…" or "The drawing paper is not ready to check."
(`useDrawingScreen.ts:419-458`). Shipping clears synchronously (`engine.ts:1191-1208`).

**D22 Stroke counting (L).** RN adds one per finger (`useDrawingScreen.ts:73-74`); shipping adds one
per stroke group (`engine.ts:689` onStrokeEnd). The install banner can appear after a single
three-finger gesture.

**Dead or unused RN code noticed.** `drawing/interactions.ts` createStrokeInput is test-only.
`DrawingScreen.tsx` (the old screen) is not mounted by ProbeApp.
`tools/tests/native-recorded-crayon7-oracle.mjs` is unused.
