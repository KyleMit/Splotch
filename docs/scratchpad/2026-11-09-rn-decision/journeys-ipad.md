## Method

| Simulator iPad Pro 12.9" (5th gen), iPadOS 26.5          | Every check in both arms. Same model and OS as the physical iPad.                                                                         |
| Simulator iPad Pro 13" (M5), iPadOS 27.0                 | Launch check and the RN landscape palette                                                                                                 |

| Physical iPad Pro 12.9" (5th gen), iPadOS 26.5, no touch | Launch screenshots in both arms, RN probe replay ink (`finger-session-2`, up to 3 contacts), crash list. Installed builds left unchanged. |

Touch came from Appium 3.6 and XCUITest W3C pointer actions, one OS gesture path for both arms. Both
arms expose identical control labels and frames, so the same scripts drove both. Media is under
`journeys-ipad/` and `shots/ipad-sim/` in the campaign scratch folder (`$C`). Scripts are in
`$C/journeys-ipad/bin/`.

**Simulator limits.** Synthetic contacts have no radius, so they cannot test a real palm. WDA runs
one action at a time, so a rotation cannot land mid-stroke. When a second pointer pauses before it
touches down, WDA draws stray lines from that pointer in **both** arms, so held-contact results are
leads to check, not verdicts. The simulator shares the host network. Simulator memory and timing do
not stand in for the device.

## Defects worth weighing

1. **Shipping cannot launch on iPadOS 27 when built with the current SDK.** Severity: blocker for
   the next shipping iPad release. It is not a user-facing defect today: the iPad runs 26.5, and
   store builds made with older SDKs are exempt. The fix is to adopt UIScene in the Capacitor shell.
   Pricing that fix belongs to the upkeep lane.
2. **RN ink invisible after a rotation.** Severity: major on the simulator. After a rotation,
   clearing and drawing records strokes (Undo enables, colour taps apply), but the paper view stays
   mis-laid-out: a flat band at the top, textured paper only at the bottom. New ink never appears,
   even after 3 s or an app switch, until a menu opens. Seen in 4 of 9 rotate → clear → draw cycles
   and in 0 of 6 cycles without a rotation. Shipping showed it 0 of 8 times. Not checked on the
   device, because the physical iPad cannot be rotated or touched by script.
3. **Shipping drops parts of multi-finger strokes** (J22), and **shipping draws stray loops on fast
   zigzags** (J-extra-1). Both are major if they reproduce with real fingers. Both come from
   synthetic simulator input, which RN handled cleanly.
4. RN memory grew by 171 MB over 200 strokes and 20 undos, against 72 MB for shipping (simulator,
   one cycle). This is a lead for the perf lane, not a finding.

## Could anything here flip the decision?

Yes, possibly. If the RN rotation defect reproduces on the device, it is a user-facing iPad failure
in RN. Shipping's multi-finger and zigzag ink loss would count against shipping if real fingers
confirm them. Shipping's iPadOS 27 launch failure blocks any new shipping iPad release until the
scene lifecycle is adopted.
