Parent Center — section activity dot · design prototype (chosen: icon badge)

A static purple dot marks sections a parent has never opened, or whose content changed since their last visit. Opening a section clears its dot with a ~200ms fade — no counts, no pulsing, no toasts. Shells recreated from ParentCenter.svelte + tokens.css; icons are the repo's own. Seeded state: Controls & Buttons, AI Art, What's New dotted. Dotted rows carry visually-hidden "new" text in their accessible name. See splotch issue #557.

On the tablet, the default-displayed section counts as seen the moment the modal opens.
